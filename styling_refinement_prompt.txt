### PHASE 2: STYLING & COLORING REFINEMENT PROMPT (QLIK CLOUD)

**Context:** The previous layout was created, but the theme and colors are stuck on default light mode. We need to force a "Dark Executive UI" look by overriding object-level styling.

**Objective:** Transform the current sheet into the "Dark CFO Cockpit" by applying specific background colors, borders, and text styling to every object.

---

### **1. GLOBAL SHEET STYLING (FORCE DARK MODE)**
1. Go to **Sheet Properties** > **Appearance** > **Styling**.
2. **Sheet Background:** Set to **Solid Color** > `#101726` (Midnight Navy).
3. **Title:** Disable "Show Title".

### **2. TOP FILTER BAR REFINEMENT**
1. Convert the Filter Panes from vertical to a single **Horizontal Filter Pane** across the top.
2. **Styling Panel:**
   - Background: `#1b263b` (Dark Blue Accent).
   - Label Color: `#FFFFFF` (White).
   - Selection Color: `#f59e0b` (Orange).
3. Disable "Show Title" for the filter object.

### **3. OBJECT-LEVEL CSS/STYLING FOR CARDS (THE "BORDER" LOOK)**
For each of the 6 KPI cards (which are **Containers** or **Text Objects**), apply these specific settings in the **Appearance > Styling** panel:

**A. Common Card Styling:**
- **Background Color:** `#1e293b`.
- **Border:** Enable. Color: `#334155`. Width: 1px.
- **Corner Radius:** 2px (Square look).
- **Title Styling:** White text, Bold, Font size 14-16px.

**B. Specific Accent Borders (Top & Left):**
*Note: If Qlik native styling doesn't allow individual side borders, use a thin "Text Object" as a decorative line at the top and left of each card.*

1. **Growth & Revenue:** Accent Color `#f59e0b` (Orange).
2. **Profitability:** Accent Color `#3b82f6` (Blue).
3. **Cash & Working Capital:** Accent Color `#10b981` (Green).
4. **Capital & Treasury:** Accent Color `#0ea5e9` (Sky Blue).
5. **Enterprise Risk:** Accent Color `#f43f5e` (Rose Red).
6. **Operational Levers:** Accent Color `#84cc16` (Lime).

### **4. TEXT CONTENT REFINEMENT**
- **Main Title ("CFO Cockpit"):** Change font color to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Sub-Titles:** Change font color to `#94a3b8`.
- **Bullet Points:** Use colored Unicode circles (●) matching the accent color of the card.
- **KPI Footer Box:** Background `#0f172a`, Border 1px solid matching accent color, Text color matching accent color.
